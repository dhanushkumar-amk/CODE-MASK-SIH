SOP-VM-001: Valve Maintenance Intervals

All manual valves in the process unit shall be inspected and lubricated
every 90 days. The inspection covers stem packing, handwheel operation,
and visible leaks. Actuated valves require a functional stroke test every
30 days to confirm full open and close travel within the rated time.

Valve inspection records must be logged in the maintenance register on
the same day as the inspection. Any valve that fails the stroke test
must be tagged out of service and reported to the shift supervisor
before the end of shift.

Critical isolation valves on the flare header are inspected monthly
without exception. Spare valve inventory is verified quarterly to
ensure replacements are available for all critical sizes.
