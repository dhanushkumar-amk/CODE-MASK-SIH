SOP-SD-002: Emergency Safety Shutdown Protocol

In the event of a gas leak or confirmed fire, initiate the emergency
shutdown sequence immediately. First, isolate the affected section by
closing the section block valves. Second, close the feed valves to stop
product flow into the affected area.

Third, notify the shift supervisor and the control room operator of the
shutdown. No restart attempt is permitted until the area has been
checked with a gas detector and declared safe by the supervisor.

Emergency shutdown drills are conducted quarterly. All operators must
attend and sign the drill attendance register. The fire extinguishers
in the affected zones are inspected monthly and their pressure gauges
must remain in the green zone.
