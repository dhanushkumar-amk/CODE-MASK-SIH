SOP-CI-004: Corrosion Inspection Procedures

Piping corrosion inspections are performed every 6 months on high-risk
lines and every 12 months on standard lines. High-risk lines are those
carrying corrosive media or operating above 80 degrees Celsius.

Inspectors check wall thickness at designated measurement points using
ultrasonic testing. Any reading below 80 percent of nominal wall
thickness requires immediate engineering review.

Minor surface corrosion should be cleaned, coated with a suitable
inhibitor, and scheduled for re-inspection within 30 days. Pitting that
exceeds 1 millimeter in depth triggers a full section inspection and
replacement planning. All findings are recorded with photographs in the
corrosion register.
